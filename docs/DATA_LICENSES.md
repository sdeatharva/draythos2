## 1. Overview and Licensing Policy

Draythos extracts, aligns, and normalizes financial terminology across mixed international data rooms without retaining copyrighted document corpora. The training and evaluation benchmarks are constructed from publicly available financial reporting taxonomies, statutory accounting standards, and regulatory disclosure glossaries.

Per Section 10.1 and Section 21 (Rule 10):
- Draythos stores **concise labels, functional definitions, and metadata provenance**.
- No bulk private documents or non-essential text passages are retained in repository artifacts.
